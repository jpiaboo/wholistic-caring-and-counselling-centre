Faith meets compassion

Wholistic Caring and Counselling Centre (WC&CC) is a religious trust-deed organisation registered in Kenya in 1994 and run as a non-profit.

Our mission is to empower individuals through education, counselling, advocacy and training, and to create a safe, supportive environment that nurtures mental health and wellness.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the centre located?

We are based in Kenya. Call or message us for directions and current visiting arrangements.

What services do you offer?

Individual, group, family and institutional counselling, plus support programmes for youth, families, older adults and people affected by substance use.

Do you offer medical services?

No. We are a counselling and support centre, and we can refer you to trusted partners when you need medical care.

Is counselling confidential?

Yes. Your privacy and dignity are central to how we work.
